- 2026-10-07: Fund-to-cabal and withdraw each publish the user's `balance_changed` hint when submitted, not only when they settle, so Home shows the in-flight amount at once.
- 2026-10-09: Detection is split from crediting. The deposit watcher records `deposit.candidate_seen` with its checkpoint, and `funding.resolve_deposit_candidate` credits it or dismisses it as `ours` or `not_deposit`. A new wallet starts at the chain tip. Fund and withdraw still spend only the canonical USDC account.
- 2026-10-09: First sight starts a new wallet at its creation time minus five minutes, not at the chain tip, so a deposit made before the watcher's first sight is credited. History before that is still not replayed.
- 2026-10-10: A cash out sale that can't start (the venue refuses the quote, or the last retry fails) ends as `trade.blocked` with a batch size of 0, so the cash out settles on the cash on hand instead of staying selling.
- 2026-10-11: M24 multichain (#4917, #4974). Deposits from Base, Arbitrum and Ethereum go to one app-owned EVM deposit wallet per user, which a Privy wallet automation forwards to the Solana member wallet; the existing watcher credits the arrival, `evm_deposit_forwards` tracks the forward and `arriving_micros` shows it. Wallet automations over deposit accounts because deposit accounts need a 1-of-1 user-owned destination. Withdrawals gain `destination_kind: evm` through Privy `/transfer`, exact input, never retried once an on-chain step confirmed.
- 2026-10-21: `Withdraw`, `FundCabal` and `CashOut` bind their `Idempotency-Key` to the row they create. A retry under the key returns that row, and resumes a `created` withdrawal or fund transfer that never reached signing, instead of creating a second one. A crash after the broadcast is the new flow 07 and 15 outcome `crash:after-broadcast` (#5822).
- 2026-10-20: An unattributed deposit no longer pauses the cabal (#5825). It is quarantined: the ledger never holds it, so pot value and shares ignore it, and a bounce that gives up resolves the row with the amount still out of the pot. Pauses opened for earlier deposits close when their deposit returns, is held or fails.
- 2026-10-21: `POST /v1/me/withdrawals` gets an actor rate limit, and `Withdraw` refuses a sixth fresh Solana destination in 24 hours with `rate_limited` before any signature, so the relayer's fee and token account rent stay bounded per user (#5828).
- 2026-10-21: `Withdraw` reserves the relayer's fee and token account rent against per-user, per-destination and global daily budgets before it signs (#5860). Past a budget the transfer is built without the account create, and past the fee too it is refused with `rate_limited`. Budget storage that fails closes the withdrawal.
- 2026-10-21: The fund settler and the EVM withdrawal poller take turns across rows. `last_attempt_at` is stamped on every visit and the lists order by it, so 100 rows that cannot move no longer lock out newer ones (#5837). Each row has its own time limit.
- 2026-10-21: `available_micros` counts only the wallet's associated USDC token account, the one fund and withdraw spend from. USDC the watcher credited into any other account of the wallet is `unspendable_micros` (still in `on_chain_micros`), and `relayer.Transfers.BuildConsolidation` moves it into the associated account. No operator command calls it yet (#5838).
- 2026-10-28: `GET /v1/cabals/{id}/fund/preview` returns the caller's share before and after a fund, computed for at most the available cash with the pot's own rounding. A settled `GET /v1/fund-transfers/{id}` returns `slice_bps`. `CabalBanned` joins flow 7's outcomes (#6414).
- 2026-10-28: `POST /v1/me/withdrawals/preview` takes an optional amount and address and always returns the network fee, the fee mode, the available cash, the most the caller can send, the minimum and the arrival time; with an amount it adds `over_micros` and `below_min`. Solana reports a fee of 0 on top, EVM reports the bridge cut included in the amount (#6415).
- 2026-11-01: `GET /v1/admin/queues/bounces` lists the external deposits still `detected`, `bouncing` or `bounce_failed`, each with its external-deposit id, instead of the open `external_deposit` pauses nothing opens any more. `monacoctl funding bounce list` prints the same rows by status, and `monacoctl funding deposit rescan <wallet> [--from <signature>]` re-scans a member wallet for a missed Solana deposit (#7502).
- 2026-11-01: A USDC transfer into a cabal treasury is not recorded as an external deposit when it lands in the arrival band of a bridge-back still `submitted` in `trade_steps`: at least the amount sent less the quoted fee, 0.10 USDC and 1%, at most the amount sent. Each bridge-back owns one such transfer, so a second transfer in its band, or one outside every band, is recorded and bounced as before. A succeeded bridge-back stores the Solana fill signature as its `arrival_tx`, and `trading.OwnsSignature` owns it (#7414).
