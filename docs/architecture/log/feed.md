# Feed & comments log

Dated record of changes to [feed.md](../feed.md). Add one line per change, newest last.

- 2026-09-29: `kind` is `trade | proposal | cabal_created | member_joined | price_move`, matching [data-model.md](../data-model.md); `ref_type` names `swaps`, not `cabal_txns`. The feed is a fifth tab, Feed, between Home and Cabals (default; see #535). The market price poller appends `asset.price_moved`; no second poller, and no push on price moves.
- 2026-09-27: Decided every cabal is public in the feed, with no private flag. Defaults applied: non-members view but do not comment on other cabals' proposals until moderation ships; moderation is report, admin removal and a per-user rate limit; `cabal_created` and `member_joined` items with no money amounts, and no funding or cash-out items; price move is change since previous close; `top` is comments plus votes over 24 h; news deferred. Proposal status comes from `governance`'s `proposal.executed` and `proposal.execution_blocked`. `asset.price_moved` has a flows row. Feed hints use the SSE hub's `global` key. Notification choices point at notifications.md. `proposal_comments` no longer migrates, since the new backend starts empty. All open questions closed.
- 2026-09-27: Reconciled with [backend-platform.md](../backend-platform.md). Owner is the `social` module, rollout step 6. Event subjects match the RFC flows table (`proposal.withdrawn`, `trade.failed`; `proposal.executed` dropped for `trade.confirmed`). Price moves come from `market`. `CreateComment` is a command with `Idempotency-Key` and `comment.created` in the same `uow.Do`. Realtime moves from `live.feed` and `/v1/feed/stream` to `hint.>` and the RFC's single `/v1/stream` hub. Payload amounts are integer micros; server renders display strings. Added open questions on new item kinds and global hint routing.
- 2026-09-26: Feed items written by the `feed` consumer on the NATS event bus; realtime hints moved from Postgres `NOTIFY` to core NATS `live.feed` ([event-bus.md](../event-bus.md)). Price moves emit `asset.price_moved`.
- 2026-09-26: Initial decision. One FeedObject table with proposal, trade, price-move and (later) news kinds. Single filterable, searchable GET. FeedComment on every item, replacing proposal comments. Realtime via SSE leaning; notifications TBD.
- 2026-10-03: The feed tables and `GET /v1/feed` and `GET /v1/feed/{id}` shipped (#590). `ref_type` for a price move is `asset_price_moves`, and each kind has exactly one `ref_type`. `cabal_name` and `symbol` are snapshot columns for search and the symbol filter. Items carry server-rendered `title`, `detail` and `tone`; the raw `payload` stays on the server. `scope=mine`, mutes and `sort=top` come later as additive changes.
- 2026-10-06: Comments shipped as flow 21 (#622). A reply to a reply is stored under the top-level comment with `reply_to_user_id` set, and `comment.created` names who a reply answers in `parent_author_id` and `reply_to_user_id`, with `parent_deleted` true when that comment is deleted. `GET /v1/feed/{id}/comments` returns one `{comment, replies}` thread per top-level comment, paged by top-level comment. A deleted comment keeps its place with a null `body` and the `body_display` "Comment deleted". `GET /v1/feed/{id}` gains `can_comment`. A create or delete hints `global.feed` after the commit.
- 2026-10-06: Proposal items shipped (#664). The `feed` durable writes a `proposal` item on `proposal.created` and moves its `status` forward through one guarded update. Ranks are open 1, passed and execution_failed 2, every other status 3 and terminal. A status never moves back, and passed to execution_failed is the one same-rank move. A status event that finds no item naks with `FeedItemPending` until the create lands. `trade.failed` counts only when its source is a proposal. The detail line is the status label, with no vote tally because `governance` emits no per-vote event. The proposer's name lives in `payload.actor_name`, so the profile-rename handler already refreshes it.
- 2026-10-06: `sort=top` shipped (#664). It ranks the items from the last 24 hours by comment count, then newest first, then id, and its cursor carries all three. Votes are not part of the rank because `governance` emits no per-vote event. A plain cursor and a top cursor are not interchangeable, and each sort refuses the other's with `invalid_input`.
- 2026-10-19: `reply` and `threshold` items shipped (#5615). A comment on a proposal item inserts a `reply` item in its own transaction, and `proposal.passed` inserts a `threshold` item whose detail stays "Execution pending". Neither takes comments, and deleting a comment deletes its `reply` item. `feed_objects`' kind and ref_type checks accept `reply`, `threshold` and `feed_comments`.
- 2026-10-28: GIFs come from GIPHY on the device behind a `GifProvider` port (#6420). The key `GIPHY_SDK_KEY` is a client key read at build time like Privy's, results are `pg-13`, 25 a page, and only `media*.giphy.com` and `i.giphy.com` media is kept. There is no GIF route on the server.
- 2026-10-28: Emoji reactions shipped as flow 21b (#6431). `PUT` and `DELETE /v1/feed/{id}/reactions/{emoji}` return the item's reaction summary and append `reaction.added` and `reaction.removed` (no consumers). The like routes now write a ❤️ row in `feed_reactions` and stop writing `feed_likes`.
- 2026-10-28: Proposal posts carry a live `tally` and `expires_at` (#6446). `GET /v1/feed` and `GET /v1/feed/{id}` read them from governance's `Tallies` port in one call per page, so nothing is stored in `feed_objects` and an undone vote needs no feed write. `vote.cast` and `vote.retracted` only publish the `global.feed` hint after commit. Social receives the port through `Wire`, because governance already imports social.
- 2026-10-28: `GET /v1/feed` and `GET /v1/feed/{id}` take `scope=network` (my cabals plus the users I follow) and `scope=others` (cabals I am not in), and the list takes `status=open` (#6455). `all` stays the default, so older builds keep working.
- 2026-10-28: A closed proposal writes one `result` item with its outcome, shape, caller and final tally (#6465). `proposal.passed`, `proposal.failed` and `proposal.expired` write it through one projection that re-renders title and body on every write, and `proposal.passed` stops writing `threshold` items. Rule-change proposals make no `proposal` and no `result` item.
- 2026-10-28: Every feed item resolves to one thread (#6479). A cabal-shaped `result`, a `threshold` and a `reply` share their proposal's thread; a proposal's thread takes replies from its cabal's members only, every other thread from anyone who can see it (founder, 10-27), which replaces "non-members cannot comment until moderation ships". `CanReply` and `CanReact` hold the two rules. `comment.created` gains `item_kind`, `thread_feed_object_id`, `item_actor_name`, `item_label`, `item_noun`, `cabal_name` and `reply_rule`.
- 2026-10-28: `GET /v1/users/{id}/activity` serves a member's votes (without their side unless it is the caller), proposals with their result, replies and months topped (#6474). It merges four sources under one `(at, kind_rank, source_id)` cursor and leaves the Feed alone. Governance gains a `MemberActivity` port that social picks in `Wire`.
