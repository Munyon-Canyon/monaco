Closed the reward, qualification, handle, minimum, window and attribution-path questions.

- 2026-10-07: The `referrals.qualify` handler on the `referrals` durable consumes `cabal.funded` and checks the phone at each funding. A funding below $10 or by a referee with an unverified phone leaves the referral `attributed`, and the next funding of at least $10 with a verified phone qualifies it; nothing sweeps when a phone is verified later. `qualified_at` is the delivery time because `cabal.funded` carries no timestamp (default; see #521, #680).
