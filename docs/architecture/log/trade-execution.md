- 2026-10-31: An EVM trade whose token amount would exceed int64 is refused with `trade_too_large` at proposal and again before `bridge_out`, because token amounts are `bigint` columns and Robinhood tokens have 18 decimals. Widening the columns to `numeric(78,0)` follows (#7455).
- 2026-11-01: Money review follow-ups (#7411). A fund into a cabal with no shares is refused (`pot_value_changed`) while the pot still holds a live cash-out, a token or cash in transit, and a landed fund waits to settle until it clears, because a mint at zero shares is priced 1:1 whatever the pot holds. A Jupiter `/execute` `Failed` with a code of -1000 or lower (failed to land, expired, timed out, unknown) is not final: Jupiter does not say the transaction can no longer land, so the swap stays `submitted` and the sweeper fails it only once the blockhash has expired and the signature is absent. Codes -1 to -5 (request refused before it was sent) and program errors still fail at once. The swap step stores the transaction it sends (`trade_steps.sent_tx`) before the send and a resume sends that, because Privy rejects a reused idempotency key with a different body. A 2xx Privy transfer response that does not decode is an unknown outcome (`trading.bridge.outcome_unknown`, step left `pending`), not a failed send. A failed `bridge_back` is alerted and retried by the poller.
- 2026-10-31: A Privy action that `failed` after a broadcast step is an unknown outcome, not money returned. The step stays `submitted`, a `bridge_out` parks its swap, `trading.bridge.outcome_unknown` alerts, and the treasury reconcile alerts on a Solana deficit that lasts two ticks (#7490).
- 2026-11-01: A routed swap with no receipt after 10 minutes parks as `swap_receipt_missing` and alerts, and `force-resolve` and `fail-parked` close it. A routed swap is leased to one runner (`swaps.lease_holder`, `lease_until`) and the poller skips it until the lease expires. A cabal's routed swaps take turns through `evm_swap_locks` from approval to confirm, so one buy's Permit2 approval is not replaced by the next (#7491).
- 2026-11-01: A Solana buy is booked from the treasury's chain delta of the bought token, and a zero or unreadable delta leaves the swap `submitted` with `fill_unread`, as for a sell. A buy's funds check no longer adds the bought token's transfer-fee headroom to the USDC it needs, because that fee reduces the tokens received. Solana swaps record `fee_micros` as null (unrecorded) instead of 0. The sweeper requires the second "not found" status read's context slot to be at or after the first (#7516).
