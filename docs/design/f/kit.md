## Three density rules

1. One job and one prominent button per screen; a button row holds two buttons at most.
2. Two lines per row, one trailing fact (a figure over its change, `.row.dense`, is one fact). No third-line class exists. Previews show four or five rows plus "See all".
3. Nothing is removed for density (founder, 10-22 late). A feature, section or control that does not fit moves behind a tap, a "See all", a menu or an i-sheet. Each move, merge, shortening or hide is listed in your notes.md with its own reason. "Density" or "less crowded" is not a reason; without one, the content stays where it was.

## Do not override, never in copy

**Do not override:** any `--token`, or any kit selector's size, radius, colour, font or spacing. In `extra.css`, add new classes for your signature object (A's seal, C's pinned section, D `.close-figure`, E's season rules, F's outside-app surfaces). No gradients, no box-shadow (the tab bar's is the only one), nothing under 13 px (Caption 2, 11, only inside the share card image and system surfaces), no caps except `.tile` and `.sh.sys` labels, no SF Mono except `.address`. At most one `.utabs` per screen; chart ranges stay plain labels.

**Never in copy:** crypto assets, "token" or "tokens" (a holding is shares: never "token-shares", "token market"), clan, group chat, discover, "propose a".

## States

**States:** the vote hold is `.vote-pair.pick`, then `.holding` with `data-progress` from 0 to 100 (add `.live` to animate 600 ms), then `.committed`, then `.voted-row`. The toast animates with `.toast.enter` / `.toast.exit`. For Dynamic Type, put `data-dt style="--dt:1.235"` on a container, and add `.ax` at accessibility sizes. For Reduce Transparency, add `.rt` on the phone.
