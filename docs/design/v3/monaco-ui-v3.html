The research rules carried into every screen
Native iOSTabView and NavigationStack for destinations; sheets for bounded tasks; system text, controls and keyboards. Glass belongs to navigation and controls, not every content block.
Consistent productionThe approved cover assets and wordmark are reused. UI text, figures and controls are typeset components, not generated inside images.
Borrow patterns, not brandsNo new moodboard, mascot, palette or competitor layout. New views inherit Home v2’s tokens.
Financial hierarchyPersonal money and group pots are distinct. Deposits are not investment gains. Approved, pending, completed, expired and blocked are different states.
Useful retentionDiscussion, identity and shared decisions—not pressure to trade. People can be followed; cabals can be viewed/requested.
First sessionSign-in and invitations preserve context; profile customization is optional. Provider checkout is not invented.
EvaluationEach screen includes relevant recovery or permission states. Native accessibility, live integrations and unseen routes still need implementation testing.

Primary implementation references: Apple’s SwiftUI design session, Materials, Accessibility, and NN/g’s prototyping research. Screen-level provenance distinguishes supplied material from proposed layouts. These principles do not prove retention or compliance.

v1.5 scope, modal decisions and exclusions

Current written comments take precedence over the meeting summary: Propose stays a Buy/Sell chooser; share links appear at bottom-left of covers. Buy/Sell are proposal actions, never instant orders. Trading bots are removed from this current-release prototype; the previous library is preserved.

Buy / SellNative confirmationDialog: two choices plus Cancel; no scrolling action list.
Review proposalA half-height sheet that never expands to full screen; sticky submit action, scrollable body with the thesis last, draft retained on dismissal.
VotesA half-height sheet that never expands to full screen, with a scrollable eligible-voter list and the same tally as its parent proposal.
Funding confirmedA compact result sheet and Done—not an alert or a separate navigation destination. Only a settled funding reads as confirmed; an accepted one reads Funding this cabal…
Filters and advanced dataPicker menus for category/time; one multi-select Feed filter; DisclosureGroup for execution details. No opaque custom SwiftUI sheet background by default.
DeferredRecommended cabals, GIFs, rich shared-link previews, stock chat, global posting, user badges, AI asset summaries and asset news modules.

All demos run locally and make no orders or account changes. Token prices, rankings and movement are fixtures, not live market facts. News is an explicit empty/offline slot. Public performance and visible holders must respect sharing permissions.
