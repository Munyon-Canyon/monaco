M8 "Mobile foundation" makes a mobile ticket dispatchable the way an M7 backend ticket is (parseable header, Implement exactly, stage 0 that builds and tests the app on a budget, stage 1 that fails a broken Swift change, merge queue that still runs mobile-core and ios), on feature branch `backend-rewrite-checkpoint-4`, tracking #508. Times are UTC on 2026-09-30. Times marked ~ are estimated.

## Decisions

### Milestone shape

- **~04:00** Every M8 ticket had no `Touches` or `Base branch` header. `monacoctl agents batch` refuses that (`batch.go` "no Touches line"). Both fields were added to 20 ticket headers.
- **~04:00** Blocked-by lines were corrected. #938 waits on #539 and #540. #939 waits on #936 and #944. #937 waits on #980 and #938. #943's parenthetical was removed. #541 was dropped from #603 and #713. Native links were resynced. `scripts/sync-blocked-by.py` reads only the first 30 links, so one stale link on #713 was removed by hand.
- **~04:10** The milestone had three conflicting exit conditions. M8 now ends at the foundation (#508's status board). The TestFlight cutover #713 moved to a new milestone "M8.5 — Mobile cutover".
- **~04:10** #540 and #941 were closed because their work had already merged on the feature branch. A merge into a non-default branch does not close an issue.
- **~04:20** Three speed tickets were filed because no launch-time, frame-time or iOS CI step budget existed. #1061 is the launch budget. #1062 is the scroll hitch. #1063 is the iOS CI step budget.
- **~04:30** The operator took over Logan's open M8 PRs. Every PR carries milestone M8, labels `milestone-m8` and `mobile`, assignees mrrobot-ec and lognorman20, and `Part of` or `Closes`. Tickets are assigned before any worker starts on them.
- **~04:30** No commit, PR body or comment carries a Co-Authored-By, Signed-off-by or "Generated with" line. The operator is the only author. This overrides standing order 7 for this milestone.

### Machine and tooling

- **~05:30** On the Intel i9 laptop three owners with parallel Docker and Xcode builds pushed load to 146. Heavy jobs run one at a time through `scripts/qa/xcode-lock.sh`. A hung Docker Desktop backend was recovered by terminating its processes and reopening the app.
- **~05:00** The mobile-core Python checks ran 17.8 s against the 15 s stage-0 row on this Intel machine. The suite was sped up to 6.8 s instead of raising the budget.
- **~05:00** xcsift has no x86_64 release binary. #936 builds it from source on Intel.
- **17:40** #1120 measured the iOS job. `Resolve Swift packages` took 383 s warm, the largest step. Build took 160 s with the compilation cache.
- **~19:30** The merge queue ejects an entry whose checks do not report within 30 minutes. With about five macOS runners, #1025 was ejected once while its checks waited for a runner. Raising the timeout needs a repo admin. The operator has Maintain.
- **20:12** #1171 landed. Merge-queue runs fetch the trunk's warm caches as artifacts. The build fell from ~680 s to 211-286 s, but the job stayed at 851-929 s because package resolution (255-262 s) and serial downloads (~180 s) remained. #1207 targets both (488 s warm on a dispatch).
- **22:21** #1201 prunes superseded mobile Actions caches after each warm run. The cache went from 10.64 GB (over the 10 GB cap) to 8.83 GB.
- **~23:00** Docker Desktop's backend hung four times (400-700% CPU with one idle container). It is capped at 4 CPUs / 4 GB and turned off when it hangs.
